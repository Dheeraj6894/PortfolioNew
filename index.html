My core stack spans C#, both .NET Framework and .NET Core, SQL Server, Entity Framework Core, and ADO.NET — with hands-on experience in Crystal Reports and integrating third-party APIs. I like backend logic that's clean enough to hand off to the next person without a walkthrough.
